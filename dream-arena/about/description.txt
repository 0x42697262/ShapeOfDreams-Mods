Host-only PvP arena for friendly matches.

Turns on player-versus-player combat, empties the arena of monsters, and keeps
the run going when players are knocked out. Free-for-all by default, with a
two-team mode.

ONLY THE HOST NEEDS THIS MOD. Everything is enforced server-side, so your
friends can join and fight with nothing installed.

Configure it from the Dream Arena button in the lobby settings, or with the
arena console commands in Developer Mode.

Proof of concept: there are no win conditions yet, so a match never ends on
its own.
