Host-only PvP arena for friendly matches.

Pick "PvP Arena" as the game mode when creating a lobby. Your run opens at the
usual campfire, where everyone is friendly - use it to regroup between rounds.
Take the rift out of camp and you arrive in the Lone King's Zenith with PvP
live: no monsters, no boss, and knocked-out players get back up so the match
keeps going. Free-for-all by default, with a two-team mode.

ONLY THE HOST NEEDS THIS MOD. Everything is enforced server-side, so your
friends can join and fight with nothing installed.

Proof of concept: there are no win conditions yet, so a match never ends on
its own.
