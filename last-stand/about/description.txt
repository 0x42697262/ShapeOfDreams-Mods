[h1]Last Stand[/h1]
[b]Endless wave survival for Shape of Dreams. Solo or co-op. Only the host needs the mod.[/b]

Hold one boss arena against waves that never stop. Every wave is bigger and stronger than the last. How far can your build go?

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Solo:[/b] press Singleplayer, then pick [b]Last Stand[/b] under Limbo.
[*][b]Co-op:[/b] create a lobby and pick [b]Last Stand[/b] in the Game Mode dropdown.
[*]Leave camp through the rift. The fight starts in the arena.
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Endless waves.[/b] The next wave starts once the last one is cleared, after a short break. Every 5th wave is a surge with twice the monsters. From wave 48, waves stop at 100 monsters that carry the whole wave's strength, and surges bring extra minibosses and Pain minibosses.
[*][b]Monsters climb the worlds.[/b] Monster stats follow the game's own world scaling: one world higher every 25 waves, and from wave 200 one world every 2 waves. Loot, gold and prices stay at your run's world.
[*][b]Minibosses[/b] every 10 waves, and [b]Pain minibosses[/b] every 20 that hurt the whole party until they fall.
[*][b]Heroic bosses[/b], the game's real zone bosses, join at wave 25, 50, 75, 100…, one more each time, and drop 2 Corrupted Chaos rewards each. If your best burst would kill all of a wave's bosses in under 90 seconds, the next ones come from a stronger world. From wave 125 they can storm in at any moment of the wave instead of last.
[*][b]Monsters from other worlds[/b] join the pool as you go. Mirage skins, hunters and hunter zones with falling artillery arrive in the late waves.
[*][b]Environments[/b] change the rules of a wave: 10% chance early, every wave from 50 to 99, then 50% (at most 2 in every 10 waves).
[*][b]Shrines on the arena's edge:[/b] Dream Well, Altar of Cleansing, Shrine of Ascension, Maw of Doom, Shrine of Destiny, Hatred Remnant and Reminiscence of Luck.
[*][b]Gold prices rise every wave[/b] (5% compounding, until wave 100), so spend it while it's cheap.
[*][b]Optional difficulty:[/b] Curse to start. Each wave waits until a player takes a curse at the Hatred Remnant, and that curse falls on everyone.
[*][b]Knocked-out heroes come back[/b] when the wave is cleared, as long as someone is still standing.
[*][b]Your score is the wave you reach.[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Fully configurable[/h2]
The host can tune almost everything from the [b]Last Stand[/b] button in the lobby's settings panel: arena room, wave size and pace, scaling, bosses, shrines, environments, item drops and more.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Host only.[/b] Friends can join on a fully vanilla client.
[*]Lucid dreams and game modifiers work as normal.
[*]The lobby shows the MOD icon, since Last Stand changes gameplay.
[*]This is an early version. Please report bugs in the comments.
[/list]
